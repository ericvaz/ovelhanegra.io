01 — The Concept

Appetite first.
Zero maintenance.

Real Kabobs. Real Good. The site opens on sizzling food photography over deep charcoal, with flame-orange action buttons that pull hungry visitors straight to Order Online. It is designed mobile-first — most people looking for dinner are on their phones — and each of your three restaurants — Manning, Lauzon and LaSalle — gets its own card with address, phone and directions.
